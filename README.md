 DylexiVision 🧠
See text differently. Read comfortably.

DylexiVision is an AI-powered reading accessibility platform designed to help people with dyslexia read, understand, and learn more comfortably. It combines AI-powered text assistance with personalized reading tools to make learning more accessible.

## ✨ Features

- 📷 **OCR Text Scanner** – Extract text from images into editable text.
- 📋 **Paste Text** – Type or paste text directly into the application.
- 🧠 **AI Text Simplification** – Convert complex passages into easier-to-understand text.
- 🌐 **Easy English & Tamil** – Simplify text in supported languages.
- 🔊 **Text-to-Speech** – Listen to text instead of relying only on reading.
- 🎤 **Karaoke Reading** – Follow along with synchronized word highlighting.
- 👁️ **Reading Focus Mask** – Focus on specific lines while reducing distractions.
- 🔤 **Syllable Highlighting** – Break words into manageable reading units.
- 📝 **Bionic Reading** – Highlight parts of words to assist visual reading.
- 🔎 **Word Explanation** – Understand unfamiliar words with AI assistance.
- 💡 **Synonyms** – Discover alternative words and meanings.
- 📌 **Key Points & Summaries** – Extract important information from lengthy text.
- 📑 **Paragraph Chunking** – Divide large passages into smaller sections.
- ❓ **Comprehension Tools** – Generate questions to support understanding.
- 🔠 **Letter Confusion Support** – Assist with commonly confused letters such as b/d and p/q.
- 📚 **My Library** – Save, edit, and revisit reading material.
- ⚙️ **Personalized Reading Settings** – Adjust fonts, sizes, spacing and reading preferences.
- 🇮🇳 **English & Tamil Support** – Designed for bilingual reading accessibility.

 🎯 Mission

To make reading and learning more accessible by using technology to reduce reading barriers and support people with dyslexia in understanding information with greater confidence.

 🛠️ Technologies

- Artificial Intelligence
- Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
- Text-to-Speech
- Web Technologies
- Accessibility-focused UI/UX
